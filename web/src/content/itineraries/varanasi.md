## Day 1 · Arrive and the evening aarti

Check in near the ghats. At dusk, watch the Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat from the steps or a boat.

> **Tip:** arrive 45 minutes early for a good spot, and keep bags close in the crowd.

## Day 2 · Sunrise boat and the old city

A dawn rowing boat past the ghats, then the old city lanes, the Kashi Vishwanath corridor and a silk-weaving workshop.

> **Tip:** photographing cremations at Manikarnika Ghat is not allowed. Please respect this.

## Day 3 · Sarnath

A half-day trip (about 30 minutes away) to the Dhamek Stupa, the archaeological museum and the Tibetan temples.

> **Tip:** the Sarnath museum is closed on Fridays.

## Day 4 · A last walk and home

A final walk along the ghats, a yoga class or a classical music lesson, then onward travel.

> **Tip:** agree boat prices before you board, and choose registered boatmen with life jackets.

## Local food

- **Breakfast:** kachori-sabzi with hot jalebi.
- **Snacks:** tamatar chaat, and Banarasi paan (ask for it without tobacco).
- **Sweet:** thick lassi in clay cups, and malaiyyo on winter mornings.

## Safety notes

- **Lanes:** the old city is a maze. Save your hotel on an offline map.
- **"Free" guides:** decline guides who lead you to shops; book through your hotel or agent.
- **Fog:** December and January fog can delay trains and flights. Keep a buffer day.
