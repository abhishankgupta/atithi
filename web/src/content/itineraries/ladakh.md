## Day 1 · Fly into Leh (3,500 m)

Do nothing. Rest and drink plenty of water.

> **Tip:** plan 48 hours of rest before going higher. Acclimatisation is not optional.

## Day 2 · An easy day in Leh

Shanti Stupa at sunset, Leh Palace and the old town — slowly.

> **Tip:** avoid alcohol for the first few days.

## Day 3 · Monasteries of the Indus valley

Thiksey for morning prayers, then Hemis and Shey.

> **Tip:** walk clockwise around stupas and prayer wheels.

## Day 4 · Leh to Nubra

Cross Khardung La to Nubra Valley for Diskit Monastery and the Hunder dunes.

> **Tip:** foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for Nubra and Pangong. Your agent arranges it.

## Day 5 · Turtuk

A day trip to Turtuk, a Balti village with apricot orchards close to the border.

> **Tip:** carry several copies of your passport and permit for checkpoints.

## Day 6 · Nubra to Pangong

A long, scenic drive along the Shyok river to Pangong Tso, with a night in a lakeside camp.

> **Tip:** nights drop below freezing even in summer. Bring a down jacket.

## Day 7 · Pangong to Leh

Sunrise over the lake, then back to Leh over Chang La.

> **Tip:** only take altitude medicine on a doctor's advice — see one before your trip.

## Day 8 · A free day in Leh

Rafting on the Zanskar in season, cafés, or the Hall of Fame museum.

> **Tip:** mobile coverage is limited; postpaid Indian SIMs work best.

## Day 9 · Fly home

A morning flight out of Leh.

> **Tip:** flights can be cancelled for weather. Keep onward plans flexible.

## Local food

- **Warming:** thukpa noodle soup and skyu, a hand-pulled pasta stew.
- **Snacks:** momos and khambir bread with apricot jam.
- **Drinks:** butter tea and sea-buckthorn juice.

## Safety notes

- **Altitude sickness:** a headache, nausea or breathlessness at rest means stop climbing, and descend if it gets worse. Get medical help.
- **Roads:** passes can close without warning. Travel with experienced local drivers.
- **Permits:** carry your permit and passport at all times.
