## Day 1 · Arrive in Kochi

Settle into Fort Kochi and walk past the Chinese fishing nets at sunset.

> **Tip:** stay in walkable Fort Kochi rather than mainland Ernakulam.

## Day 2 · Fort Kochi and Mattancherry

Jew Town, the Paradesi Synagogue, spice warehouses and the Dutch Palace. In the evening, a Kathakali performance — arrive early to watch the make-up.

> **Tip:** some sites, including the synagogue, close on Fridays and Saturdays. Check timings.

## Day 3 · Kochi to Munnar

A scenic 4–5 hour drive into the Western Ghats with waterfall stops.

> **Tip:** the hill roads twist a lot — take motion-sickness tablets if you need them.

## Day 4 · Munnar

The tea museum, Eravikulam National Park and walks through the plantations.

> **Tip:** winter mornings are cool (10–15°C). Pack a warm layer.

## Day 5 · Munnar to Thekkady

Drive to Periyar. A spice-garden tour, then an evening show of Kalaripayattu, Kerala's martial art.

> **Tip:** book Periyar boat rides and guided walks at the official counters.

## Day 6 · Alleppey houseboat

Board around noon and cruise the backwaters, with meals cooked on board and a night on the water.

> **Tip:** choose a licensed houseboat with life jackets and a fire extinguisher, and ask to see them.

## Day 7 · Varkala

Drive to Varkala (about 3 hours) for cliff-top cafés, the beach and an Ayurvedic massage.

> **Tip:** currents are strong. Swim only in flagged zones near lifeguards.

## Day 8 · Trivandrum and home

Visit Kovalam beach or the Padmanabhaswamy Temple, then fly home.

> **Tip:** the temple has a strict dress code and entry may be restricted for non-Hindus. Check before you go.

## Local food

- **Breakfast:** appam with stew, or puttu and kadala curry.
- **Backwaters:** karimeen pollichathu (pearl-spot fish baked in banana leaf).
- **Must try:** a sadya — a vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf.

## Safety notes

- **Monsoon:** June to August brings heavy rain and occasional landslides in the hills. Follow local advisories.
- **Beaches:** rip currents are common. Never swim alone or at night.
- **Wildlife:** stay with your guide in forests and don't feed monkeys.
