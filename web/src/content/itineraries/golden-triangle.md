## Day 1 · Arrive in Delhi

Airport pickup, or a prepaid or app taxi to your hotel. Rest, then an easy evening at Humayun's Tomb or Lodhi Garden.

> **Tip:** most international flights land late at night, so keep day one light.

## Day 2 · Old and New Delhi

A cycle-rickshaw through Chandni Chowk, Jama Masjid and the spice market. In the afternoon, Qutub Minar and India Gate, then Dilli Haat for crafts.

> **Tip:** cover shoulders and knees at mosques and temples. A light scarf is useful.

## Day 3 · Delhi to Agra

About 3.5 hours by expressway. Agra Fort in the afternoon, then the Taj Mahal at sunset from Mehtab Bagh across the river.

> **Tip:** the Taj Mahal is closed every Friday — plan around it.

## Day 4 · Taj at sunrise, then Ranthambore

Enter the Taj Mahal at opening for soft light and smaller crowds. Stop at Fatehpur Sikri, then continue to Ranthambore by road (about 5 hours) or train.

> **Tip:** buy Taj tickets only at the official counter or the Archaeological Survey of India's website.

## Day 5 · Tiger safari

Morning and afternoon jeep or canter safaris in Ranthambore National Park, with the hilltop fort in between.

> **Tip:** safaris are booked in advance on the official forest portal. Carry your passport.

## Day 6 · Jaipur

Drive to Jaipur (about 3.5 hours). Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar, then Johari Bazaar for textiles and block prints.

> **Tip:** agree auto-rickshaw fares before you ride, or use an app cab.

## Day 7 · Amber Fort and home

An early visit to Amber Fort and the Panna Meena ka Kund step-well, then drive or fly to Delhi for your flight.

> **Tip:** skip elephant rides up to the fort — walk or take a jeep.

## Local food

- **Delhi:** parathas in Old Delhi, chaat, butter chicken, jalebi.
- **Agra:** petha (a translucent sweet) and a bedai-kachori breakfast.
- **Jaipur:** dal baati churma, laal maas, pyaaz kachori and thick lassi.

## Safety notes

- **"Closed today" scams:** if a stranger says a monument or your hotel is closed and offers to take you elsewhere, decline politely and check yourself.
- **Transport:** use airport prepaid taxi counters or app cabs, and share your trip with someone at home.
- **Air quality:** November to January can bring smog to Delhi. Carry an N95 mask if you're sensitive.
