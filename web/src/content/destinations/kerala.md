Kerala is green, gentle and easy to travel. Combine colonial Fort Kochi, the tea estates of Munnar, wildlife in Periyar, a night on a backwater houseboat and the beaches of Varkala.

The monsoon (June to August) is lush but wet; Ayurveda centres consider it the best season for treatments.
