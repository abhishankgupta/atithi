The Golden Triangle links India's capital with two of its most famous cities. It's the easiest first trip: good roads, plenty of hotels, and the Taj Mahal at its heart.

- **Delhi:** Old Delhi's lanes and the Mughal tombs of New Delhi.
- **Agra:** the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort.
- **Jaipur:** the pink city's palaces, forts and bazaars.

Add Ranthambore for tigers, or Varanasi for a spiritual contrast.
